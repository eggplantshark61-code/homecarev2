No. 06Questions, Anticipated

Asked before. Answered here.

Q.1

What does care cost, and will insurance take part?

+
The fee follows the plan — its hours and its depth — and is quoted in writing after the complimentary assessment. Care of this kind is customarily private pay; many long-term-care insurance policies, however, reimburse it. Bring the policy to our first meeting. We will read it together and prepare whatever documentation a claim requires.
Q.2

How are your caregivers chosen?

+
Sparingly. Each candidate passes a background check, reference verification, and an interview conducted in person. Those who join are then matched to a household — by skill first, and by temperament with equal seriousness. A match that does not sit well is remade at a word.
Q.3

May the schedule change as circumstances do?

+
It is expected to. Families begin with a few hours a week and extend to overnight, twenty-four-hour, or live-in arrangements as needed — then draw back again when strength returns. One conversation with your coordinator is sufficient.
Q.4

Which towns do you attend?

+
Oakville, where we are based; Watertown; Waterbury; and the towns of Litchfield County. If you sit at the edge of that map, telephone us — we accept a household only when we can serve it faithfully, and we will tell you plainly either way.
