A letter from the founder

on why HarborLight exists at all

When my own family needed help at home, I learned what most families learn the hard way — that finding good care means a dozen phone calls, a rotating cast of strangers, and nobody who seems to know your name. I promised myself that if I ever built a care company, it would be the one I wished we’d found.

HarborLight is that company. We are new, we are local, and we intend to stay small enough that I personally know every caregiver we send and every family we serve. Our caregivers are background-checked, our credentials are in order — licensed, insured, and bonded — and our plans flex from a few hours a week to full round-the-clock, live-in care.

But the thing I am proudest of is simpler: when you call us, one person answers, knows your story, and stays with you for the whole journey. That is how neighbors treat each other. That is how we will treat you.

— The HarborLight FamilyFounder & Owner · Oakville, Connecticut

Questions our readers ask most

plain answers, the kind we’d want ourselves

What does care cost — and will insurance or an LTC policy help?

Your rate depends on the hours and kind of help in your plan, and after the free assessment you’ll receive it in writing — clear, itemized, no surprises. Many long-term care insurance policies reimburse services like ours; bring us your policy and we’ll happily help you read the fine print and file the paperwork. Traditional Medicare typically does not cover non-medical home care.

How do you choose and screen your caregivers?

Slowly, and on purpose. Every caregiver completes a full background check and reference verification, then sits for a personal interview with the owner. Only then do we think about matching — pairing skills and temperament to your household, and supervising the match continuously once care begins.

Can the schedule bend as our needs change?

That’s rather the point. Begin with a few hours a week; grow to overnights, weekends, or full 24-hour and live-in care as life requires. One call to (860) 555-0100 — to the one person who knows your file — adjusts everything.

Where exactly do you serve?

We’re headquartered in Oakville and serve Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury and the towns of Litchfield County. If you’re near the edge of that map, write to us anyway — we’d rather say “yes, gladly” or “no, but here’s who can” than leave you wondering.
