Things people ask us

What's this going to cost? Does insurance cover it?+

Depends on how many hours you need — that's the honest answer, and we'll put an exact number in writing after your free visit. No surprise fees, no padding. And yes: many long-term care insurance policies cover home care like ours. Bring us your policy and we'll help you read the fine print and file the paperwork.

Who exactly is coming into my parents' house?+

Somebody we'd trust in our own parents' house. Every caregiver passes a full background check, reference checks, and a face-to-face interview before they meet a single client. Then we keep supervising — spot checks, check-ins, the works. And you meet them before care ever starts.

What if our needs change next month?+

Then the plan changes next month. Start with four hours on Tuesdays, ramp up to overnights, scale back after recovery — whatever life throws. One phone call to your coordinator and it's sorted. No contracts trapping you into hours you don't need.

Do you cover my town?+

We serve Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury and the Litchfield County towns around them. We keep the map small on purpose — it's how caregivers show up on time and supervisors actually supervise. On the edge? Call us. We'll tell you straight, and point you somewhere good if we can't help.
