Clear answers

Wondering about
the practical side?

How is cost handled — and will insurance pitch in?
Rates depend on hours and level of care, and you’ll get a clear written quote right after your free assessment. Many long-term care insurance policies cover services like ours — bring your policy along and we’ll walk through it together, paperwork included.
How carefully are caregivers screened?
Very. Every caregiver clears a full background check, reference verification and in-person interviews before joining us — and then works under ongoing supervision. HarborLight is licensed, insured and bonded.
Can we change the schedule as life changes?
Anytime. Start light with a few hours a week and grow to daily visits, overnights or full 24/7 live-in care — or dial back as things improve. One call to your coordinator updates the whole plan.
Which areas do you cover?
We serve Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury and communities throughout Litchfield County, Connecticut. Nearby but not sure? Give us a ring — if we can’t serve you brilliantly, we’ll say so.
