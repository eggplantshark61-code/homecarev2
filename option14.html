Gentle answers

The things families ask us first.

What will care cost — and can insurance soften it?
Your rate depends on the hours and kind of support in your plan, so we quote after the free visit — in writing, with nothing hidden. Home care of this kind is usually private pay, but many long-term-care insurance policies reimburse it. Bring your policy to the visit; we'll read it with you and help with the paperwork if it applies.
How do you choose the people you send into our home?
Slowly and carefully. Every caregiver is background-checked, reference-verified, and interviewed face to face. Then we match — on skills first, and just as deliberately on warmth, patience, and shared interests. If the chemistry isn't there, tell us once and we'll rematch without fuss.
Can the schedule breathe with us as needs change?
Yes — that's the design. Families begin with as little as a few hours a week and can grow to overnight, 24-hour, or live-in care. Scale up after a surgery, ease back when strength returns. One call to your care contact is all it takes.
Where does HarborLight serve?
We're rooted in Oakville and care for families across Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury, and the towns of Litchfield County. If you're near the edge of our map, call — if we can promise reliable, unrushed visits to your address, we'll happily say yes.
