Good questions

Getting to the roots of it

The questions families ask before they call — answered the way we’d want them answered ourselves.

What will care cost — and can insurance help?

Every plan is priced by its hours and level of help, and after your free assessment you receive the numbers in writing — nothing hidden under the leaves. Many long-term care (LTC) insurance policies reimburse care like ours, and we gladly help you review your policy and handle the claim paperwork. Standard Medicare usually does not cover non-medical home care.

How are your caregivers chosen and screened?

Carefully, and never quickly. Each caregiver passes a thorough background check and reference verification, then meets the owner face to face. Only after that do we consider matching them to a family — and we keep supervising long after the first visit.

Can the schedule grow or shrink with our needs?

Like anything living, yes. Start with a few hours a week; expand to overnights, weekends, or full 24-hour and live-in care whenever life asks for more. One call to (860) 555-0100 reshapes the plan.

Which towns are within your reach?

Our roots are in Oakville, and our branches reach Watertown, Waterbury and the towns of Litchfield County. Live just beyond? Ask anyway — we answer honestly, and quickly.
