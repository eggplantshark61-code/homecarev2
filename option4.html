Our care

Eight quiet ways we make home feel easier

From a warm dinner to a full night's watch, care is shaped around your routines — never the other way around.

Personal Care

Respectful help with bathing, grooming, dressing and moving safely around the house — always at your loved one's pace, always preserving dignity.

Companionship

Conversation over coffee, a hand of cards, a walk when the light is good. Loneliness fades when someone genuinely looks forward to visiting.

Homemaking & Meals

Light housekeeping, laundry, and meals that actually get eaten — cooked the way home is supposed to smell.

Medication Reminders

Gentle, reliable prompts so the right dose happens at the right hour — including those easy-to-miss evening rounds.

Respite for Family Caregivers

You have been carrying a lot. Step away for an afternoon — or a week — knowing someone steady is holding the line.

Transportation & Errands

Doctor visits, the pharmacy, the good grocery store, church on Sunday. A safe ride and a helpful arm, door to door.

24-Hour & Live-In Care

For families who need someone awake through the small hours — or living in — we build around-the-clock coverage with carefully coordinated caregivers, so the night is never uncertain.

Recovery & Post-Hospital Support

The first weeks home after a hospital stay decide everything. We handle discharge instructions, follow-ups, meals and mobility so healing stays on course.

How it begins

From first call to first calm evening

No pressure, no obligation — just a clear, unhurried path to the right care.

1

A free in-home assessment

We sit at your kitchen table — with your loved one, and with you — and listen. Routines, worries, wishes, the things that make home home. It costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

2

A personalized care plan

We shape a written plan around real life: the 7 a.m. coffee, the Tuesday hair appointment, the evening medications. You review it, adjust it, and approve every line.

3

A carefully matched caregiver

Skills matter; so does chemistry. We introduce a background-checked caregiver chosen for temperament and interests as much as experience — and if it isn't a fit, we rematch, simple as that.

4

Ongoing supervision & family updates

Care is never set-and-forget. We supervise continuously, adjust the plan as needs shift, and keep the family honestly informed — so distance never means darkness.

Good questions

Asked by nearly every family we meet

What does in-home care cost, and will insurance help?

Cost depends on the hours and level of support your care plan calls for, so we quote it plainly after your free assessment — no surprises, no bundled extras. Many long-term care insurance policies reimburse in-home care, and we'll help you review your policy and handle the paperwork it requires.

How do you screen your caregivers?

Every caregiver passes a thorough background check, reference verification and in-person interviews before they ever enter a client's home. Then we go a step further: we match for personality and interests, supervise ongoing visits, and stay accountable to you through one consistent point of contact.

Can the schedule flex as our needs change?

Yes — that's the point of a living care plan. Start with a few hours a week and grow to overnight, 24-hour or live-in coverage as needs evolve, or scale back after recovery. One call to your care coordinator is all it takes to adjust.

Which towns do you actually serve?

We're based in Oakville and serve Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury and the surrounding towns of Litchfield County. We deliberately stay local — it's how we keep visits punctual and supervision personal. On the edge of our map? Call us and we'll give you an honest answer.
