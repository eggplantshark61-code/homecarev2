06Questions — asked and answered
6.1What does home care cost, and does insurance help?+

Rates depend on the hours and level of support in your plan, and you receive exact numbers in writing after the free assessment — never a surprise invoice. Many long-term care (LTC) insurance policies reimburse our services, and we help you review your policy and prepare the paperwork. Medicare generally does not cover non-medical home care.

6.2How are caregivers screened?+

Every caregiver passes a comprehensive background check, reference verification, and a face-to-face interview with the owner. Then we match on skills and temperament for your specific household — and we supervise continuously after placement.

6.3How flexible is the schedule?+

Completely. Start with a few hours a week and scale to overnights, weekends, 24-hour rotations or live-in care. Plans are reviewed regularly, and one call to (860) 555-0100 adjusts anything.

6.4Which towns do you serve?+

We are based in Oakville, CT and serve Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury and communities across Litchfield County. On the edge of the map? Ask — short answers, honest ones.
