Before you set out

Questions families ask before the first visit.

If yours isn't here, call (860) 555-0100 — a real conversation beats a webpage every time.

How is care priced, and will insurance help cover it?

Pricing follows the plan, not the other way around: once your free assessment sets the hours and level of support, we provide clear written rates with no hidden fees. Many long-term care insurance policies reimburse for our services, and we'll help you review your policy and prepare the documentation your carrier needs.

How are your caregivers screened?

Every caregiver clears a comprehensive background check, professional reference verification and face-to-face interviews before joining us. We then match by personality and interests as deliberately as by skill — and supervise every engagement with scheduled check-ins and family updates.

Can the schedule change as our needs change?

Yes — that flexibility is the point. Families often begin with a few hours a week and adjust upward after a hospitalization, or downward as independence returns. From occasional visits to 24-hour and live-in care, the plan moves with the tide of your needs, without changing companies mid-stream.

Which communities do you serve?

We're anchored in Oakville and serve Watertown, Waterbury and towns throughout Litchfield County. If you're just beyond that range, call us anyway — where we can say yes, we will.
