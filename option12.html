The questions everyone asks first

What will it cost — and does insurance chip in?

It depends on hours and the kind of help, which is exactly what the free visit figures out — then you get clear, written pricing with zero surprises. Good news: many long-term care insurance policies cover services like ours, and we'll happily help you decode your policy and file the paperwork. we're weirdly good at insurance forms

Who exactly will be coming into our home?

Someone we'd trust in our own homes. Every caregiver passes a full background check, reference checks and in-person interviews. Then we match by personality — hobbies, humor, energy — and introduce them to your family face-to-face before any care starts. If it's not a great fit, we re-match, no hard feelings.

Can we start small and change the schedule later?

Absolutely — most families do. Start with a few hours a week for errands and company, then adjust up or down as things change. We cover everything from occasional visits to full 24-hour and live-in care, and switching levels never means switching companies.

Do you come to our town?
