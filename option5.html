Our philosophy

Good care does not announce itself. It is simply there — punctual, unobtrusive, and entirely dependable.

HarborLight is a new, locally owned practice built on an old conviction: that a person's home is their finest room, and they should remain in it — comfortably, safely, and on their own terms. We keep our client list deliberately small. Each family is known to us by name, and each is served through a single, consistent point of contact.

At home, as it should be

In practice

Small courtesies, kept daily.

The newspaper folded the way she likes it. The good teacup, not the travel mug. A caregiver who knows which chair catches the morning sun — and who remembers, without being asked, that Thursday is the day her sister calls.

These are not extras. In our view, they are the work itself. Care that ignores the particulars is merely supervision; care that honors them is hospitality.

Enquiries

Matters worth clarifying.

What will care cost, and is insurance accepted?+

Fees follow the care plan — nothing more, nothing hidden. After your complimentary assessment, we present the figure plainly and in writing. Many long-term care insurance policies reimburse services such as ours; we will gladly review your policy's provisions and prepare whatever documentation it requires.

How are your caregivers vetted?+

Rigorously, and in person. Comprehensive background checks, verified references and unhurried interviews precede any engagement. Thereafter, supervision is continuous — vetting, in our view, is not an event but a practice.

May the schedule change as circumstances do?+

Of course. Care may begin with a few hours each week and extend to overnight, twenty-four-hour or live-in arrangements — or contract again as recovery allows. A single call to your coordinator sets any change in motion.

Which communities do you serve?+

Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury and the towns of Litchfield County. We confine ourselves to this circle deliberately; proximity is what permits punctuality and personal oversight. If you sit near its edge, telephone us — you will receive a straight answer.
