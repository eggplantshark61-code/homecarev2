Questions, answered plainly

The details families ask about first.

If your question isn't here, call (860) 555-0100. A person answers — not a phone tree.

What does home care cost, and does insurance help?

Cost depends on the hours and level of support in your plan, so we quote after the free assessment — in writing, with no surprise line items. Non-medical home care is typically private pay; however, many long-term-care insurance policies reimburse it, and we help families document care for LTC claims. We'll review your policy language with you before you commit to anything.

How are caregivers screened?

Every caregiver passes a background check, reference verification, and an in-person interview before joining HarborLight. They work under a supervisor, follow your written care plan, and are covered by our insurance and bonding. If a match ever feels wrong — for any reason — we rematch promptly.

How flexible are schedules?

Very. Families start anywhere from a few hours a week to full 24/7 or live-in coverage, and adjust as needs change — after a hospital discharge, during a family trip, or as a condition progresses. There's no long-term contract standing between you and the right schedule.

Which towns do you serve?

We're based in Oakville and serve Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury, and communities throughout Litchfield County. Near the edge of that area? Call us — if we can staff your home reliably, we'll say yes; if we can't, we'll say so honestly.
