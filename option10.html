Fair questions

Things families ask us at the kitchen table.

What does home care cost, and can insurance help?

Cost depends on how many hours you need and the kind of help involved — which is exactly what the free assessment sorts out. We'll give you clear, written pricing with no surprises. Many long-term care insurance policies cover our services, and we'll gladly help you read your policy and handle the paperwork.

How do you choose your caregivers?

Slowly and carefully. Every caregiver passes a thorough background check, reference checks and in-person interviews before they ever meet a client. Then comes the part we're pickiest about: matching. We pair caregivers to families by personality and interests as much as by skills — and we supervise the relationship the whole way through.

Do we have to commit to a big schedule?

Not at all. Some families want a few hours a week for errands and company; others need 24-hour or live-in care. Start where you're comfortable — the plan can grow, shrink or shift as life does. You'll never be locked into more than you need.

Which towns do you serve?

Our home base is Oakville, and we serve Watertown, Waterbury and communities throughout Litchfield County. On the edge of that map? Give us a call at (860) 555-0100 — we'd rather say yes than make you guess.
