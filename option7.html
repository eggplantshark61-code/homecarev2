Straight answers

Questions families ask first

What does home care cost, and does insurance help?
Pricing depends on hours and level of care, and we quote it plainly after your free assessment — no bundles you don’t need. Many long-term care insurance policies cover our services, and we’ll help you review your policy and handle the claim paperwork.
How do you screen caregivers?
Every caregiver passes a full background check, reference verification, and in-person interviews before joining HarborLight. We’re licensed, insured, and bonded, and every caregiver works under ongoing supervision with regular quality visits.
Can the schedule flex as needs change?
Yes — that’s the point of the plan. Start with a few hours a week and scale to daily, overnight, or full 24/7 live-in coverage whenever you need to. One call to your care coordinator updates everything.
Which towns do you serve?
We’re based in Oakville, CT and serve Oakville, Watertown, Waterbury, and communities throughout Litchfield County. Just outside that area? Call us — we’ll tell you honestly whether we can cover you well.
